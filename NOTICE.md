## Made for assetc

Every other file in `samples/` was made for this repository and is not taken
from elsewhere: the procedurally generated images (`sky.exr`,
`fx/spark.atlas/`, `ui/icons.atlas/`, `water/lake*.png`), the
`props/sword.gltf` model, the water shader (`shaders/water.frag.wgsl` and
its compiled `water.frag.spv`), and every assetc source file (`.amat`,
`.acmat`, `.ashader`, `.amod`, `.anoise`, `.agradient`, `.apalette`,
`atlas.toml`, `.aschema`, `.adata`, `.agraph`, `.atree`).

The GUI's own fonts and icons are credited in the app (Help ▸ Licenses,
see [`docs/features/gui.md`](docs/features/gui.md#open-source)).
